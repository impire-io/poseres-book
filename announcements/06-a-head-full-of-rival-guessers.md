## LinkedIn

PRA does not contain a model of the world. It contains a crowd of them.

Each member is called a frame, and a frame is a bet: this world can be described with D knobs. A three-knob frame and a fourteen-knob frame live in the same head at the same time, watching the same stream of experience, each insisting on its own answer. Every frame does two jobs on every step. It re-expresses what it just saw as a setting of its own knobs, then predicts where those knobs will land after the next action. The real next observation does the grading itself.

Nobody ever gets edited. If the system suspects a frame's size is wrong it spawns a rival at the new size and lets the two fight it out, so every structural change is reversible by default: a bad idea loses and gets evicted, and what already worked was never touched. Newborns get a protected childhood before judgment day. Past that, the more crowded the head gets, the harsher the survival bar becomes.

Run it on a small rover world and the population breathes. Fifteen, nineteen, thirteen frames on three runs of the same world, with no line in the code asking for any of those numbers.
