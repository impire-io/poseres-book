## LinkedIn

Watch a baby in a high chair drop a spoon over the side. Clatter. An adult picks it up and returns it. It gets dropped again. By the fifth drop the adult has theories about the baby's motives, and none of them are charitable.

What the baby has after five drops is five records that share the same three parts. What things were like, what I did, what things were like next. Before, action, after. That shape is the only input my system ever gets. No textbook, no labels, no instructions.

The middle part is what makes the record worth anything. Every morning the rooster crows and then the sun comes up, and to a pure watcher "the crow causes the dawn" and "the crow merely comes first" look identical. You settle it by keeping the rooster quiet for one morning and seeing whether the sun still rises. Cause and effect can only be observed from one vantage point, and that is the inside of something that acts.

One thing is missing from the record on purpose. There is no score in it, no reward, no "that was good." A reward is somebody's opinion baked into a number, and whoever picks it has decided in advance what the machine is for.
